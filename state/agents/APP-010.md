## Summary

Streamlit **Simulation** page compares per-game home-win probabilities from
XGBoost (`daily.jsonl`), Monte Carlo simulation (`simulation.jsonl`), and market
no-vig prices. Includes slate table, grouped `st.bar_chart` comparison, and
per-game expanders with total-runs summaries plus optional histogram/quantile
charts. Graceful empty state when `simulation.jsonl` is missing.
